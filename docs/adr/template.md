# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-...md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces this decision? The technical, product, or security pressures in play. State
the facts and constraints, not the conclusion.

## Decision

The choice, stated plainly in the active voice: "We will …".

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the costs and the
follow-on obligations, not just the benefits.
