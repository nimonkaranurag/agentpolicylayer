# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers responsible for enforcement at
<https://github.com/nimonkaranurag>. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.

For the full set of community standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
enforcement guidelines, see the linked Contributor Covenant v2.1 text above.
